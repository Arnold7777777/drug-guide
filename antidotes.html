☠️ Antidotes & Overdose

The reversal agents you get tested on, plus the 121 drugs in your guide that carry a documented overdose treatment.

🚨 The three that show up most

acetaminophen → acetylcysteine

opioids → naloxone

benzodiazepines → flumazenil

If you remember nothing else on this page, remember these three pairs.

⭐ Hyperkalemia — the order they ask for

1. Calcium gluconate→ 2. Insulin + D50→ 3. Albuterol→ 4. Kayexalate→ 5. Dialysis

Calcium is first because it stabilizes the myocardium. It does not lower the potassium.

🛡️ Antidote pairs to memorize

28 pairs. The search box at the top filters these too.

💊 acetaminophen (Tylenol)

🛡️ acetylcysteine

Give within 8–10 hr for best effect. Watch the rising AST/ALT, not the symptoms — the client can look fine while the liver is failing.

💊 opioids (morphine, fentanyl, heroin)

🛡️ naloxone

Shorter half-life than most opioids — it can wear off and the client re-sedates. Keep monitoring, be ready to redose.

💊 benzodiazepines (lorazepam, midazolam)

🛡️ flumazenil

Can trigger seizures in chronic users or mixed TCA overdose. Use cautiously.

💊 warfarin (Coumadin)

🛡️ vitamin K (phytonadione)

Add fresh frozen plasma or PCC when the bleeding is active — vitamin K alone is too slow.

💊 heparin

🛡️ protamine sulfate

1 mg protamine per ~100 units heparin. Monitor aPTT.

💊 enoxaparin (Lovenox)

🛡️ protamine sulfate (partial)

Only reverses about 60% — low-molecular-weight heparin is not fully reversible.

💊 dabigatran (Pradaxa)

🛡️ idarucizumab (Praxbind)

Direct thrombin inhibitor, its own dedicated antidote.

💊 rivaroxaban / apixaban

🛡️ andexanet alfa

Factor Xa inhibitors. Very expensive, not stocked everywhere.

💊 digoxin

🛡️ digoxin immune Fab (DigiFab)

Suspect toxicity with anorexia, nausea, yellow-green halos. Hypokalemia makes toxicity worse.

💊 magnesium sulfate

🛡️ calcium gluconate

Classic in OB. First sign of trouble is loss of deep tendon reflexes, then respiratory depression.

💊 iron

🛡️ deferoxamine

Urine turns a reddish 'vin rosé' color as iron is chelated out.

💊 lead

🛡️ succimer, edetate calcium disodium (EDTA)

Chelation. Check the source of exposure before discharge.

💊 organophosphates / insecticides

🛡️ atropine + pralidoxime

SLUDGE symptoms — salivation, lacrimation, urination, defecation, GI upset, emesis.

💊 cyanide

🛡️ hydroxocobalamin, or the cyanide kit

Suspect it after enclosed-space smoke inhalation. Turns urine and skin red.

💊 carbon monoxide

🛡️ 100% oxygen (hyperbaric if severe)

Pulse ox reads falsely normal — you must draw a carboxyhemoglobin level.

💊 methotrexate

🛡️ leucovorin (folinic acid)

Called 'leucovorin rescue'. Hydrate and alkalinize the urine.

💊 ethylene glycol / methanol

🛡️ fomepizole (or ethanol)

Antifreeze. Blocks alcohol dehydrogenase so the toxic metabolite never forms.

💊 beta blockers (metoprolol, atenolol)

🛡️ glucagon

Glucagon bypasses the blocked beta receptor. Atropine first for the bradycardia.

💊 calcium channel blockers

🛡️ IV calcium + glucagon + high-dose insulin

Verapamil and diltiazem overdose drops both rate and pressure.

💊 tricyclic antidepressants

🛡️ sodium bicarbonate

Watch for a widening QRS — that is the sign to treat, not the level.

💊 neuromuscular blockers (rocuronium)

🛡️ sugammadex, or neostigmine + glycopyrrolate

Sugammadex directly encapsulates rocuronium and vecuronium.

💊 anticholinergics

🛡️ physostigmine

'Mad as a hatter, red as a beet, dry as a bone, hot as a hare, blind as a bat.'

💊 insulin / sulfonylureas

🛡️ dextrose 50% (D50), glucagon

Conscious and able to swallow? 15 g fast carbs, recheck in 15 min.

💊 volatile anesthetics + succinylcholine

🛡️ dantrolene

Malignant hyperthermia. Earliest sign is a rising end-tidal CO2, not the fever.

💊 vasopressor extravasation (norepinephrine)

🛡️ phentolamine

Infiltrate the site. Stop the infusion, leave the catheter in to aspirate first.

💊 alteplase / thrombolytic bleed

🛡️ aminocaproic acid

Stop the infusion first. Have blood products ready.

💊 potassium (hyperkalemia)

🛡️ calcium gluconate first

Calcium protects the heart, it does not lower the potassium. Then insulin+D50, albuterol, Kayexalate, dialysis.

💊 isoniazid

🛡️ pyridoxine (vitamin B6)

Also given routinely with INH to prevent peripheral neuropathy.
