🌿 Herbal Supplements

Clients take these and often do not mention them. These are the ones that actually change how their medications work.

🚨 Ask about supplements in every medication reconciliation

Most clients do not consider herbs to be medicine, so they will not list them unless you ask directly.

Ask: "Do you take any vitamins, herbs, teas or anything from the health food store?"

Supplements are not FDA-regulated for potency or purity — the dose in the bottle may not match the label.

⭐ The 4 G's — the bleeding herbs

Garlic Ginger Ginkgo Ginseng

All four increase bleeding risk — stop them before surgery. The catch: ginseng decreases warfarin's effect while the other three increase bleeding. That reversal is a favorite exam question.

💉 Stop before surgery

SupplementStop beforeWhy
Ginkgo1–2 weeksBleeding
Garlic7–10 daysBleeding, low glucose
Ginseng7 daysBleeding, hypoglycemia, BP swings
Ginger~1 weekBleeding
St. John's Wort5+ daysAlters anesthetic metabolism

🌿 The supplements

🌿 St. John's Wort

Hypericum perforatum
✅ Used For

• Mild to moderate depression

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Photosensitivity

• GI upset, dry mouth, dizziness

• Insomnia, restlessness

🔀 Drug Interactions

• The biggest interaction offender in all of herbal medicine. It is a strong CYP450 inducer, so it speeds up the breakdown of many drugs and makes them stop working.

• Reduces: oral contraceptives (breakthrough pregnancy), warfarin, cyclosporine, digoxin, HIV protease inhibitors, anticonvulsants, statins

• Serotonin syndrome when combined with SSRIs, SNRIs, triptans, tramadol, MAOIs

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Ask about it in every medication reconciliation

• Never stop or start it without telling the prescriber

• Stop at least 5 days before surgery

🌿 Ginkgo biloba

Ginkgo
✅ Used For

• Memory and cognition

• Peripheral vascular disease, intermittent claudication

• Tinnitus, vertigo

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Bleeding

• Headache, dizziness, GI upset

• Allergic skin reaction

• Seizures at high doses

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Increases bleeding risk with warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel, heparin and NSAIDs

• Lowers the seizure threshold — caution with anticonvulsants

• May raise blood pressure with thiazide diuretics

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• One of the 'G' herbs that make you bleed

• Stop 1-2 weeks before surgery

• Avoid in seizure disorders and in pregnancy

🌿 Garlic

Allium sativum
✅ Used For

• Hyperlipidemia

• Hypertension

• General cardiovascular support

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Bleeding

• Body and breath odor

• GI upset, heartburn

• Allergic reaction

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Increases bleeding with anticoagulants and antiplatelets

• May lower blood glucose — watch with insulin and oral antidiabetics

• Reduces saquinavir levels

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• A 'G' herb — bleeding risk

• Stop 7-10 days before surgery

• Watch for hypoglycemia in diabetics

🌿 Ginger

Zingiber officinale
✅ Used For

• Nausea and vomiting, including pregnancy and motion sickness

• Osteoarthritis pain

• Dyspepsia

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Heartburn, GI upset

• Bleeding at high doses

• Mouth irritation

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Bleeding risk with anticoagulants and antiplatelets

• May lower blood glucose

• May potentiate antihypertensives

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• A 'G' herb — bleeding risk

• Stop about 1 week before surgery

• Generally considered one of the safer supplements at culinary doses

🌿 Ginseng

Panax ginseng
✅ Used For

• Fatigue and energy

• Stress and immune support

• Type 2 diabetes adjunct

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Insomnia, nervousness

• Hypertension, tachycardia

• Headache

• Mastalgia, vaginal bleeding

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Decreases the effect of warfarin — the exception among the G herbs, which usually increase bleeding

• Additive stimulation with caffeine and stimulants

• Hypoglycemia with insulin and antidiabetics

• Hypertensive crisis with MAOIs

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• A 'G' herb, but it works the opposite way on warfarin — a favorite test point

• Stop 7 days before surgery

• Avoid in uncontrolled hypertension

🌿 Echinacea

Purple coneflower
✅ Used For

• Prevention and treatment of colds and upper respiratory infection

🪦 Adverse Effects

• GI upset

• Rash and allergic reactions

• Hepatotoxicity with long use

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Reduces the effect of immunosuppressants — cyclosporine, corticosteroids, tacrolimus

• May add to hepatotoxicity with methotrexate, amiodarone, ketoconazole

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Avoid in autoimmune disease, HIV, tuberculosis and transplant clients — it stimulates the immune system

• Do not use longer than about 8 weeks

• Cross-allergy with ragweed, daisies and chrysanthemums

🌿 Kava

Piper methysticum
✅ Used For

• Anxiety

• Insomnia

• Muscle relaxation

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Severe hepatotoxicity, including liver failure

• Sedation, drowsiness

• Scaly rash with long use

• Extrapyramidal symptoms

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Additive sedation with benzodiazepines, opioids, alcohol, barbiturates

• Compounds hepatotoxicity with acetaminophen, statins, methotrexate

• May worsen Parkinson symptoms with levodopa

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Banned in several countries for liver failure. Monitor LFTs.

• Never combine with alcohol

• Do not drive after taking it

• Stop 24 hours before surgery

🌿 Valerian

Valeriana officinalis
✅ Used For

• Insomnia

• Anxiety and restlessness

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Morning grogginess

• Headache, dizziness

• Rare hepatotoxicity

• Withdrawal symptoms if stopped abruptly after long use

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Additive CNS depression with benzodiazepines, barbiturates, opioids, alcohol, anesthesia

• May increase the effect of other sedatives

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Taper rather than stopping suddenly

• Tell anesthesia before surgery — it interacts with anesthetics; stop about 1 week before

• No driving after a dose

🌿 Feverfew

Tanacetum parthenium
✅ Used For

• Migraine prophylaxis

• Arthritis, fever

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Bleeding

• Mouth ulcers with chewed leaves

• GI upset

• Rebound headache on abrupt discontinuation

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Bleeding risk with anticoagulants, antiplatelets and NSAIDs

• NSAIDs may also reduce its effectiveness

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Taper, do not stop abruptly — rebound headaches

• Stop 1-2 weeks before surgery

• Avoid in pregnancy — it can stimulate contractions

🌿 Saw Palmetto

Serenoa repens
✅ Used For

• Benign prostatic hyperplasia symptoms

🪦 Adverse Effects

• GI upset

• Headache

• Decreased libido

• Mild bleeding risk

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Additive effect with finasteride and alpha blockers

• May interfere with hormone therapy and oral contraceptives

• Mild bleeding risk with anticoagulants

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Can lower PSA and mask prostate cancer — tell the provider before PSA testing

• Does not shrink the prostate; it treats symptoms

• Stop 2 weeks before surgery

🌿 Black Cohosh

Actaea racemosa
✅ Used For

• Menopausal hot flashes and night sweats

• PMS, dysmenorrhea

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Hepatotoxicity

• GI upset

• Headache, dizziness

• Rash

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Adds to hepatotoxicity with acetaminophen and statins

• May interfere with hormone therapy and tamoxifen

• May potentiate antihypertensives

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Monitor LFTs; stop for jaundice, dark urine or right upper quadrant pain

• Avoid in pregnancy, breastfeeding, and hormone-sensitive cancers

• Limit to about 6 months

🌿 Echinacea-free: Milk Thistle

Silybum marianum
✅ Used For

• Liver support, hepatitis, cirrhosis adjunct

• Some mushroom poisoning protocols

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Mild GI upset and laxative effect

• Allergic reaction in ragweed-sensitive clients

🔀 Drug Interactions

• May affect CYP-metabolized drugs

• May lower blood glucose — watch with antidiabetics

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Generally well tolerated

• Still requires LFT monitoring if used for liver disease

• Not a substitute for treating the underlying cause

🌿 Melatonin

N-acetyl-5-methoxytryptamine
✅ Used For

• Insomnia and jet lag

• Shift-work sleep disorder

• Delayed sleep phase

🪦 Adverse Effects

• Morning drowsiness

• Headache, dizziness

• Vivid dreams

• Mild hypothermia

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Additive sedation with CNS depressants

• May increase bleeding with anticoagulants

• Fluvoxamine markedly raises melatonin levels

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Take 30-60 minutes before bedtime, in a dark room

• Lower doses often work better than higher ones

• No driving after taking it

🌿 Turmeric / Curcumin

Curcuma longa
✅ Used For

• Inflammation and arthritis pain

• Dyspepsia

🪦 Adverse Effects

• GI upset

• Bleeding at high doses

• Gallbladder contraction

🔀 Drug Interactions

• Bleeding risk with anticoagulants and antiplatelets

• May lower blood glucose

• May reduce iron absorption

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Avoid in gallstones and bile duct obstruction

• Stop 2 weeks before surgery

• Poor oral absorption without pepper or fat

🌿 Glucosamine / Chondroitin

 
✅ Used For

• Osteoarthritis pain, especially the knee

🪦 Adverse Effects

• GI upset

• Headache

• Rash

🔀 Drug Interactions

• May increase INR with warfarin

• May raise blood glucose — monitor in diabetics

⚠️ Warnings & Teaching

• Shellfish allergy — many products are shellfish-derived

• Monitor glucose in diabetics and INR on warfarin

• Takes 4-8 weeks to show any effect
